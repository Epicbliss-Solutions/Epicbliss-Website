Microsoft PowerAutomate Projects

PowerAutomate Project

Multi Level Approval Flow

    Epicbliss offers a comprehensive range of Microsoft PowerAutomate solutions to streamline and enhance business processes across various industries. Our services include developing customized PowerAutomate flows and applications. We have delivered a multi-level approval process for leave requests in SharePoint, automated via Power Automate.
  • The process starts with an employee submitting a request in SharePoint.
  • Power Automate detects the request and sends it to a pre-approver for initial review.
  • If the pre-approver rejects it, a rejection email is sent to the employee, and the SharePoint request is updated.
  • If approved, a pre-approval email is sent, and the request is forwarded to the final approver.
  • At the final stage, if the request is rejected, a final rejection email is sent, and the SharePoint status is updated.
  • If approved, an approval email is sent, and SharePoint reflects the final approval.
    • This workflow ensures a structured, automated, and efficient vacation approval process
